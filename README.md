## 🌿 Overview & Design Philosophy

Prapti Dental Clinic Pvt. Ltd. is a tranquil boutique dental studio in Basundhara, Kathmandu. Designed with a genuine, calming, and clean aesthetic:
- **Authentic Nepalese Imagery:** Showcasing a serene Kathmandu boutique clinic suite and the authentic portrait of Dr. Barun Giri.
- **Single Direct Call Action:** A single prominent header Call button (`tel:+9779841930218`) for instantaneous direct telephone calls.
- **Genuine & Streamlined Sections:**
  1. **Home / Hero:** Peaceful Basundhara studio operatory with real-time open status pill.
  2. **The 4 Pillars of Prapti:** Compassionate touch, biocompatible materials, unhurried care, and family dental wellness.
  3. **Curated Treatment Menu:** Detailing gentle restorative, preventive, and cosmetic options.
  4. **Dr. Barun Giri:** In-depth clinical profile with direct phone calling and official TikTok link.
  5. **Patient Stories:** Genuine testimonials from local families in Basundhara, Maharajgunj, and Tokha, linked to Google reviews.
  6. **FAQ:** Answers addressing common patient concerns regarding comfort and visiting hours.
  7. **Visit Studio:** Address details, hours, and interactive map embed.
